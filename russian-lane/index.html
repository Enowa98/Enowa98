Your Russian keyboard

On Windows, switch layouts with Win + Space or Alt + Shift. The small grey letter shows which English key it sits on. Tap a key to hear it, or type here with the Russian layout on and watch the keys light up.

Two of your songs are Ukrainian

«Срібний пульсар» and «Твій промінь» by SadSvit are in Ukrainian, a different language from Russian. Most letters are the same, but Ukrainian adds і ї є ґ and drops ы э ъ ё. If you see і in a title, it isn't Russian.
